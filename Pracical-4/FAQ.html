Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is StudentHub? ▼

StudentHub is an online portal for managing attendance, assignments, results, notices, and student profiles.

2. How do I register? ▼

Go to the Register page, fill in your details, and submit the registration form.

3. How do I log in? ▼

Open the Login page and enter your registered email and password.

4. Where can I check my results? ▼

You can view your results from the Dashboard after logging in.

5. Who should I contact for help? ▼

Visit the Contact page or contact the college administration for assistance.
