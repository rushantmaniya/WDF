About StudentHub

StudentHub is an advanced portal for managing college activities, student profiles, attendance, and more. It provides a centralized platform for both students and faculty to access academic resources seamlessly.

Developed as part of the Web Development Frameworks course to demonstrate dynamic UI components like modal popups, image sliders, collapsible accordions, and a light/dark theme toggle.
